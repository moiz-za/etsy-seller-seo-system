# Operations Guide — May 2026

This file is universal — it applies to ANY shop, ANY product type. Nothing is hardcoded to a specific shop or product format.

## 1. Fee Structure

| Fee | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Listing fee | $0.20 | Charged on publish; renews every 4 months or on each sale |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% | Applied to item price; for physical orders ALSO applied to shipping price + gift wrap |
| Payment processing (US) | 3% + $0.25 | Per transaction |
| Payment processing (UK) | 4% + £0.20 | Per transaction |
| Payment processing (EU) | 4% + €0.30 | Varies slightly by country |
| Payment processing (CA) | 3% + CA$0.25 | Per transaction |
| Payment processing (AU) | 3% + A$0.25 | Per transaction |
| Offsite Ads | 15% or 12% | 15% under $10K/yr; 12% over; mandatory above $10K/yr |
| Currency conversion | 2.5% | When listing currency ≠ payment account currency |
| Regulatory operating fee (UK/EU) | 0.25%–1.1% | Varies by country — applies to all transactions in addition to other fees |

**Fee example A — $15 digital download (US seller):**
- Listing: $0.20 · Transaction (6.5% of $15): $0.98 · Processing (3% of $15 + $0.25): $0.70
- **Total fees: ~$1.88 · Payout: ~$13.12** (~12.5% of revenue)

**Fee example B — $25 physical item + $5 shipping (US seller):**
- Listing: $0.20 · Transaction (6.5% of $30 item+shipping): $1.95 · Processing (3% of $30 + $0.25): $1.15
- **Total fees: ~$3.30 · Payout on item: ~$26.70** (~13% of revenue)

**Pricing rule:** always absorb at least 15–20% in fees. If on Offsite Ads (mandatory above $10K/yr revenue): absorb 25–30% when an offsite-attributed sale occurs.

**ChatGPT Instant Checkout eligibility:** only sellers enrolled in Offsite Ads have listings eligible for ChatGPT Instant Checkout discovery. Opting out of Offsite Ads (only possible under $10K/yr) means losing that channel.

## 2. Shop Setup — Universal

### Must Complete All
- Shop name: memorable, niche-relevant, no trademarked terms, no celebrity names
- Shop icon: professional brand mark (500×500px min)
- Shop banner: high-quality (3360×840px)
- About section: brand story, process description, personal photos — feeds shop quality score
- Shop announcement: processing time, current promotions, anything time-sensitive
- Location: filled in accurately — impacts trust signals AND shipping eligibility

### Policies (Complete All — Universal)
- Returns & exchanges: state policy clearly. For digital: non-refundable once downloaded. For physical: accept/decline returns, specify timeframe.
- Processing time: "Instant" for premade digital downloads; realistic days for physical/custom
- Privacy policy: GDPR compliance required for EU buyers (data handling disclosure)
- Custom orders: enable IF the shop accepts them; otherwise leave disabled. State turnaround expectations clearly.

### Tax & Compliance
- US sellers: Etsy collects sales tax for marketplace facilitator states. Sellers responsible for income tax reporting.
- EU sellers: VAT applies. Marketplace VAT collection rules differ for B2C vs B2B and by country.
- UK sellers: VAT registration threshold £90,000/yr (2026). Below that, optional but cannot reclaim input VAT.
- Etsy automatically collects VAT on digital goods sold to EU consumers under the EU's VAT on digital services rules — sellers don't need separate registration for VAT-MOSS in most cases.
- Income tax: 1099-K (US) issued at $5,000+ in 2026 (lowered threshold), or earlier in some states.

## 3. Star Seller Program

**Qualification window: previous 3 months.**

| Metric | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Message response rate | 95%+ within 24 hours (initial message to a buyer-initiated conversation) |
| On-time dispatch rate | 95%+ — for digital downloads with instant delivery this is automatic |
| Review rating | 4.8+ average over the qualification window, minimum 5 reviews in window |
| Minimum activity | $300 in sales OR 5 orders in the 3-month window |

**Practical notes:**
- For digital download shops: on-time dispatch is automatic when listing type is set to "Digital download" with instant delivery. The bottleneck for Star Seller is usually response rate or rating, not dispatch.
- For physical shops: late dispatch is the most common Star Seller killer. Pad your processing time rather than promise tightly.
- Star Seller badge appears on the listing and adds a CTR uplift (Etsy doesn't publish numbers, but observed 3–8% uplift in studies).

## 4. Review System — Updated March 13, 2026

**The old 12-month rolling average no longer applies.**

### New System: Lifetime Recency-Weighted Average
- All lifetime reviews count — not just last 12 months
- Newer reviews carry more weight than older ones
- Each review's impact is **halved every year** it ages
- Star Seller eligibility still uses last 3 months only — unchanged

### What This Means
- Consistent recent excellence steadily improves your score
- A wave of recent bad reviews significantly impacts rating even with years of good history
- First 10 reviews are disproportionately important — they form your initial reputation baseline
- Respond professionally to ALL reviews — future buyers read seller responses, and Etsy's algorithm reads response presence

### Review Rules
- Never incentivize, request specific star ratings, or exchange reviews — policy violation, ban risk
- Never respond to negative reviews with hostility — keep responses brief, professional, solution-focused
- For digital downloads: if a buyer reports file issues, offer a solution (resend, alternative format, refund) before they escalate to a case
- For physical: address the specific issue (sizing, color, damage) and offer remedy

## 5. Cases & Disputes — Updated March 24, 2026

### Case Filing Threshold (Changed)
- **Old rule:** Buyers could file a case 1 day after estimated delivery
- **New rule:** Buyers must wait **7+ days past estimated delivery** for late delivery cases
- Buyer must contact seller via "Help with Order" before any case can be opened
- Open cases directly hurt search ranking — resolve quickly

### For Digital Downloads
- "Item not received" cases for instant downloads usually indicate the buyer can't find their files
- Resolution: guide them to Etsy account → Purchases → Download button
- Most digital "cases" can be resolved in one message — but the case remains on record affecting shop score until closed

### For Physical Items
- "Not as described" is the most common physical case — usually photo/text accuracy issues
- Pro-active resolution: offer a partial refund or replacement before the buyer escalates
- Tracking uploaded promptly is a strong defense in shipping-delay cases

### Open-Case Threshold
- Etsy now factors open-case rate (not just total cases) into shop quality score
- 1+ open case for more than 7 days = active suppression of search visibility
- Resolve cases within 48 hours when possible

## 7. Metric Diagnostic — Which Number Is Hurting You?

If sales are flat, identify which metric is the actual problem before changing anything. Use this lookup:

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action layer |
|---|---|---|
| Zero/near-zero impressions | SEO problem (tags over 20 chars, wrong keyword, miscategorized, IP suppression) | Re-run keyword research (SKILL Phase 3), verify tag chars, check category |
| Impressions but CTR < ~1% | Hero image weak / title unclear / price too high | Rewrite hero mockup (NOT skill scope); test lower price |
| CTR healthy (>1.5%) but conversion < ~1% | Description weak / mockups not specific enough / few reviews / price-value mismatch | Rewrite description Block 2 (Features) and Block 3 (What's Included); add more mockup photos |
| Strong conversion but low repeat traffic | Listing only ranks for one narrow query | Add tag variety (different buyer-intent angles); refresh secondary keywords |
| Sales drop suddenly with no change | Algorithm refresh / competitor undercut / seasonal cycle / open case suppressing | Check open cases first; then compare against seasonal baseline |
| Reviews dropping below 4.8 average | Recency-weighting amplifying recent issues | Respond to all recent negatives professionally; address root cause |

**Always identify the layer BEFORE applying a fix.** Rewriting the title won't help a listing whose problem is the hero image — and vice versa.
