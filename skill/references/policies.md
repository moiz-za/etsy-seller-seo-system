## 1. Creativity Standards — What Can Be Sold

**Last major update: June 10, 2025.** Etsy classifies every listing under one of these creativity buckets. The bucket determines what disclosures and limits apply.

### Designed by a Seller
Original designs created by the seller, whether sold as digital downloads or as physical handmade items.
- **Originality Requirement (August 11, 2026 Enforcement Update):** Items produced using computerized tools (Cricut, CNC, laser cutters, sublimation printers) must rely on the seller's own original creative design. Listing un-modified third-party templates, generic clipart, or pre-made cut files bought from others no longer qualifies under "Designed by a seller".
- **AI-Assisted Creations:** Allowed IF the seller prompted, directed, and contributed to the output.
- **Listing Configuration for AI Items:**
  - Dropdown settings: Who made it ➔ *"I did"*, When ➔ *"Made to order"* (or 2020-2026), What it is ➔ *"Finished product / Digital file"*.
  - **Description Disclosure:** Must include a concise, factual disclosure statement in the listing description (e.g., *"Created by the seller using AI generation tools and refined for vector cutting/printing"*).
- **Process Audit Trail:** Sellers should keep an audit trail (original prompts, vector source files, sketches) to prove originality if flagged by automated Etsy moderation.
- PLR (Private Label Rights) files: only listable if your specific license explicitly permits resale on Etsy — verify the license terms in writing before listing.
- Production partners: required to be disclosed in the listing (see §5).

### Made by a Seller (Handmade Physical)
Physical items made by the seller's own hand or with the seller's tools/equipment.
- Designed AND made by seller, or designed by seller + made with disclosed production partner
- Materials list must be accurate
- Modifications to mass-produced items do NOT qualify as handmade

### Vintage
Items 20+ years old at time of listing.
- Must be authentic — modern reproductions don't qualify even if styled "vintage"
- Estimated date of origin recommended in description
- Vintage-fur garments are NO LONGER exempt from the Aug 11, 2026 fur ban

### Craft Supplies
Tools, materials, and ingredients used to create or assemble items.
- Can be commercial — does not have to be handmade or vintage
- Must be intended for crafting/making, not finished consumer goods

### What Does NOT Qualify
- Reselling another designer's SVG/PNG files without explicit Etsy-resale permission in their license
- Offering digital services (coaching, therapy, tutoring) — prohibited even when delivered digitally
- Metaphysical services without a delivered file
- Crowdfunding, donation, or referral code listings
- Drop-shipped finished goods that are not handmade by seller or designed-by-seller with disclosed production partner

## 2. Digital Goods Rules (apply when product is a digital download)

### Delivery
- Files must be the seller's original work (or licensed for resale)
- Instant download: files deliver automatically after purchase
- Custom/made-to-order digital: state turnaround time in listing; deliver via Etsy Messages
- If unable to deliver: notify buyer and issue full refund immediately
- Files over 20MB: deliver via Google Drive / Dropbox link in order message; state this in description

### Reviews (digital + physical)
- Digital: buyers can review for **100 days from first download** OR **12 months from purchase** — whichever comes first
- Physical: 100 days from delivery
- Buyers can edit reviews within the window
- Reviews can include photos or videos

### Returns & Refunds (digital)
- Digital items are **non-refundable once downloaded** — state this in shop policies and listing
- EU/UK buyers: 14-day right of withdrawal may apply but typically not to digital downloads after the buyer accepts the loss of withdrawal right; check current EU consumer-rights guidance
- Sellers may cancel and refund at their own discretion

## 3. Physical Goods Rules (apply when product is shipped)

### Materials & Description
- Materials and dimensions listed must match the actual product
- Color variations must be photographed honestly — no enhanced saturation that doesn't match reality
- Size guides required where applicable (apparel, jewelry rings, etc.)

### Shipping
- Set realistic processing times — late dispatch is penalized in search ranking
- US shipping: keep price at $6 or below — over $6 reduces search visibility (2026 algorithm)
- Free shipping improves conversion and search ranking
- Upload tracking on all orders; required for orders ≥ $250 (US)

### Production Partner Disclosure
- If anyone other than the seller (or seller's listed employees) physically makes the item, that party is a Production Partner
- Production Partners must be added in shop settings AND disclosed in the listing
- Failing to disclose a production partner is a creativity standards violation

## 4. Prohibited Items

### Absolutely Prohibited
- Weapons, controlled substances, hazardous materials
- Child sexual abuse material
- Human remains
- Counterfeit or stolen items
- Items promoting hate speech or violence

### Animal Fur Ban (Effective August 11, 2026)
Policy updated April 2, 2026. Enforcement begins August 11, 2026.
- Products made from animal fur are banned across all categories — **no vintage exemption**
- Faux/synthetic fur products are NOT affected
- Digital files depicting fur designs are NOT affected — this ban is for physical fur products only

### Conditionally Restricted
- Adult content: designated adult-gated categories only
- Metaphysical services: only if a digital file (e.g., reading transcript, chart PDF) is delivered to buyer
- Live plants and seeds: subject to phyto-sanitary rules per destination country
- Food/edibles: subject to FDA / local equivalent labelling requirements

## 5. Intellectual Property — Universal Rules

### Hard Rules (apply to every listing)
- **Never use trademarked characters, brand names, or franchise names** in designs, titles, tags, descriptions, or attributes — Disney, Marvel, Harry Potter, Star Wars, Pokémon, Hello Kitty, NFL, NBA, college team names, etc.
- **Never use celebrity names or likenesses** in designs or listings
- **"Inspired by" does NOT protect you** — an IP complaint will still be filed and Etsy will remove the listing
- **Fan art does NOT grant permission** — you need a license from the IP holder
- **Public domain ≠ free to use commercially in all jurisdictions** — verify per country

### Common Trap Categories
- Sports team names, mascots, league names
- Music lyrics, song titles (separately copyrighted)
- Famous quotes attributed to deceased public figures may still be protected by right-of-publicity heirs
- Trademarked phrases ("That's hot", "Let's Get Ready to Rumble", etc.) — assume any catchphrase you recognize is trademarked
- Character silhouettes (Mickey Mouse ears, Darth Vader helmet shape, etc.) — visual likeness counts

### For AI-Generated Designs
- Verify your AI tool's terms regarding commercial use and ownership before listing
- DALL-E, Midjourney, Grok, Gemini, Stable Diffusion each have different commercial use terms — check them per tool, per tier (free vs paid often differs)
- Output that resembles a specific living artist's style may still trigger an IP complaint even if technically generated
- Output containing identifiable brand elements (logos, mascots) is never safe to sell

### What Happens When a Complaint Is Filed
1. Listing deactivated immediately — no prior warning
2. Seller receives automated email
3. Repeat strikes → permanent shop closure
4. Counter-notice process available if claim is wrong — file within 10 business days
5. For high-stakes disputes: consult an IP attorney before responding

### Research Tools Before Listing
- US trademarks: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks
- US copyright catalog: https://cocatalog.loc.gov
- EU trademarks: https://www.euipo.europa.eu
- UK trademarks: https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark
- WIPO global brand database: https://branddb.wipo.int

## 6. Listing Content Accuracy

- All listing text and images must accurately represent the product
- Do not claim compatibility with software the files don't actually work in (e.g., "Cricut compatible" requires actual Cricut-compatible files)
- Do not claim "commercial use" if the source license does not permit it
- Do not list features the buyer won't receive
- Listings must not violate Etsy's Discrimination and Hateful Content Policy

---

## 7. Community Behavior (April 2026 Update)

- Sellers must not use Etsy Messages, Favorites, or Collections to aggregate content in harmful ways or harass other sellers
- Review-manipulation (coordinated fake reviews, review-swap rings, paid reviews) carries permanent ban risk
- Brigading another shop (organized negative reviews from non-buyers) violates community policy
- Off-platform contact information (Instagram handles, personal websites with payment, "DM me to order direct") in listings violates Etsy Terms

---

## 8. Audit Checklist Before Publishing

Run this against every new or rewritten listing:

- [ ] No trademarked names anywhere in title, tags, description, or attributes
- [ ] No celebrity names or recognizable likenesses
- [ ] Materials/files listed match what the buyer will actually receive
- [ ] Commercial use claim (if made) is backed by actual license rights
- [ ] AI disclosure present if any AI tool was used in creation
- [ ] Production partner disclosed in shop settings (if physical + made by anyone other than seller)
- [ ] Category and attributes match the actual product
- [ ] Shipping price under $6 (US physical) or "Digital download" set correctly
- [ ] No off-platform contact details
- [ ] No claimed compatibility with software files don't actually work in

If any check fails, fix before publishing. A single policy violation can cost the new-listing recency boost (or close the shop).
